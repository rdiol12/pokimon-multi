# New areas

Large new places to explore, each with NPC quests, trainers, secrets and
wild Pokémon. Every area is generated by a script in `tools/areas/`
(`python3 tools/areas/<area>.py`), which writes its layout, maps, scripts,
trainers and encounters and registers them in the shared tables.

## Elderwood Forest

An ancient rainforest east of Fortree. A guide on the east side of Fortree
City takes you there once you have Winona's badge.

- Four treehouses joined by a canopy walkway; ladders lead up from the
  forest floor. The Elder and a ranger lookout can be visited.
- Quest: find the Elder Tree's three keepers (Bounsweet in the tall grass,
  Rowlet on the walkway, Grookey by the shrine). The Elder gives a Miracle
  Seed, 5 Sitrus Berries and TM Giga Drain, and Zarude (Lv45) appears in a
  hidden grove behind a Cut tree in the west woods.
- Trainers: Ranger Ivy, Bug Maniac Hal, Aroma Lady Fern.
- Items: 4 item balls, 4 hidden items (one up on the walkway). Wild
  Pokémon Lv33–38 (Rowlet, Bounsweet, Tropius, Heracross, Pinsir,
  Yanmega…), fishing and surfing in the pond.

## How the maps are made

- `tools/areas/tiles.py` renders any tileset or layout to PNG.
- `tools/areas/synth.py` turns a terrain sketch (`T` trees, `.` ground,
  `"` tall grass, `~` water…) into metatiles by learning from Hoenn's own
  maps of the same tileset, so forest edges and canopies join up the way the
  original maps do.
- `tools/areas/build.py` has the `Area` builder (sketching, stamps copied
  from real maps, 3x3 edge sets like ponds, events) and registers maps,
  layouts, scripts, region map sections, encounters and trainers.
- Screenshots: `python3 tools/areas/shots/run.py tools/areas/shots/<area>.lua OUT`
  (needs a built ROM and libmgba-dev). `Driver.flag(name, on)` sets any flag.

## Area flags and trainers

- Area flags live in `SaveBlock3` (`struct AreaSaveData`, 1024 flags) at
  ids from `AREA_FLAGS_START` (0x6000), so they don't use up the game's
  regular flags. Flags, object visibility and hidden items all work with
  them. Each area gets 24 flags (`register_constants`).
- Area trainers have their own table (`src/data/area_trainers.party`) with
  ids from `AREA_TRAINERS_START` (0x400), and their beaten flags are area
  flags, so `TRAINERS_MAX` doesn't need raising. Each area gets 8 slots.
