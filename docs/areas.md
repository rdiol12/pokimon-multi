# New areas

Large new places to explore, each with NPC quests, trainers, secrets and
wild Pokémon. Every area is generated by a script in `tools/areas/`
(`python3 tools/areas/<area>.py`), which writes its layout, maps, scripts,
trainers and encounters and registers them in the shared tables.

## Elderwood Forest

An ancient rainforest east of Fortree. A guide on the east side of Fortree
City takes you there once you have Winona's badge.

- Four treehouses joined by a canopy walkway; ladders lead up from the
  forest floor. The Elder and a ranger lookout can be visited.
- Quest: find the Elder Tree's three keepers (Bounsweet in the tall grass,
  Rowlet on the walkway, Grookey by the shrine). The Elder gives a Miracle
  Seed, 5 Sitrus Berries and TM Giga Drain, and Zarude (Lv45) appears in a
  hidden grove behind a Cut tree in the west woods.
- Trainers: Ranger Ivy, Bug Maniac Hal, Aroma Lady Fern.
- Items: 4 item balls, 4 hidden items (one up on the walkway). Wild
  Pokémon Lv33–38 (Rowlet, Bounsweet, Tropius, Heracross, Pinsir,
  Yanmega…), fishing and surfing in the pond.

## Ember Springs

A hot-spring town in a volcanic valley above Lavaridge. A hiker in
Lavaridge Town leads you up once you have Flannery's badge.

- Pokémon Center, an inn (free rest) and a hermit who gives a Flame Orb.
- The spring attendant offers a soak: it heals the party and raises every
  Pokémon's friendship (`Area_SoakInHotSpring`, src/area_specials.c).
- Quest: three Slugma are plugging the steam vents on the west, north and
  east hills (wild battles). The Spring Keeper gives a Soothe Bell and TM
  Overheat, and his son stops guarding the Old Spring, where a lady gives
  a Charcadet Egg and offers an extra-strong soak.
- Trainers: Kindler Blaze, Beauty Cindy, Hiker Rocco, Expert Saki.
- Items on the vent hills, hidden items, Lv29–34 wild Fire types (Numel,
  Litleo, Salandit, Rolycoly, Sizzlipede, Charcadet, Turtonator, Heatmor).

## Cinder Caverns

A volcano dungeon under Ember Springs. The cave door is on the north hill
of Ember Springs.

- 1F: a lava river splits the cave, so you go round by the sides. A
  volcanologist explains the valves. Kindler Ignatius, Hex Maniac Rosa.
- B1F: a lava lake. Three old Team Magma valves (west, east and far
  south-west) each cool a stretch of the causeway across the lake
  (`setmetatile`, reapplied on load from FLAG_CINDER_VALVE_*). Hiker Bruno,
  Cooltrainer Tess. Magmarizer, TM Flamethrower, Full Restore.
- Molten Heart: after the Pokémon League the guard on the landing steps
  aside, and Heatran (Lv50) waits on an island in the lava. If you defeat
  it, it sinks back into the magma; if you run, it stays.
- Wild Pokémon Lv33–40 (Slugma, Torkoal, Magby, Litwick, Carkol, Larvesta,
  Heatmor…) and rock smash encounters.
- Map ids for bosses and events: MAP_CINDER_CAVERNS_1F, _B1F, _HEART.

## Skyrope Canyon

Rock mesas cut by narrow trails, and a deep river gorge crossed by three
long rope bridges. A hiker in Fallarbor Town leads you there once you have
Norman's badge.

- Quest: a gust tore the planks off the east bridge and scattered the
  Bridgewright's three coils of rope (west trail, north-west basin, the
  south grass). Bring them back and he mends the bridge (`setmetatile`,
  reapplied on load from FLAG_SKYROPE_BRIDGE_FIXED). Across it is a ledge
  where Hawlucha (Lv36) trains, with Black Belt Koji, a Rare Candy and a
  Sharp Beak. The artist gives a Star Piece once the bridge is fixed.
- Trainers: Hiker Hamish, Bird Keeper Wren, Picnicker Daisy, Black Belt
  Koji. Breakable rocks, Strength boulders, a wandering Mudbray and a
  Rufflet guarding its nest.
- Items (TM Aerial Ace, Hard Stone, Max Repel) and three hidden items.
  Wild Pokémon Lv30–35 (Rufflet, Vullaby, Drilbur, Sandile, Mudbray,
  Gligar, Skarmory, Hawlucha…), Basculin in the river, rock smash
  encounters.
- The style learns from Routes 114 and 115. Route 114's wooden bridge is
  the rope bridge. synth.py now gives each metatile the collision it has
  in its sketch class, since Route 114 uses the same rock as floor and wall.

## How the maps are made

- `tools/areas/tiles.py` renders any tileset or layout to PNG.
- `tools/areas/synth.py` turns a terrain sketch (`T` trees, `.` ground,
  `"` tall grass, `~` water…) into metatiles by learning from Hoenn's own
  maps of the same tileset, so forest edges and canopies join up the way the
  original maps do. Styles (`tools/areas/styles.py`) pick the real maps to
  learn from; with `colors=True` open ground is split into `g` grass, `s`
  sand and `r` bare rock by colour.
- `tools/areas/build.py` has the `Area` builder (sketching, stamps copied
  from real maps, 3x3 edge sets like ponds, events) and registers maps,
  layouts, scripts, region map sections, encounters and trainers.
- Screenshots: `python3 tools/areas/shots/run.py tools/areas/shots/<area>.lua OUT`
  (needs a built ROM and libmgba-dev). `Driver.flag(name, on)` sets any flag.

## Area flags and trainers

- Area flags live in `SaveBlock3` (`struct AreaSaveData`, 1024 flags) at
  ids from `AREA_FLAGS_START` (0x6000), so they don't use up the game's
  regular flags. Flags, object visibility and hidden items all work with
  them. Each area gets 24 flags (`register_constants`).
- Area trainers have their own table (`src/data/area_trainers.party`) with
  ids from `AREA_TRAINERS_START` (0x400), and their beaten flags are area
  flags, so `TRAINERS_MAX` doesn't need raising. Each area gets 8 slots.
